## Part 1: Citizen User Manual (WIP)

This section is for residents who want to report issues in their neighborhood.

### Creating an Account
1. Open the app and click the button 'Register here' to sign up.
2. Enter your full name, preferred username, and create a secure password.
3. Once your account is created, you will be taken to back to login where you can enter your account credentials.

### Filing a New Report
To start a report, click the **Submit a New Report** button on your dashboard. You must fill out all the fields below before you can finish:

1. **Personal Information:** Enter your full name and your active contact number so the department can reach you.
2. **Select Incident Type:** Use the menu to choose the category that fits the problem:
    * Emergency and Disaster
    * Security and Public Order
    * Public Repairs
    * Sanitation
    * General Concern
3. **Describe the Issue:** Type a clear description of the problem (example: "Large pothole in the middle of the road").
4. **Set Exact Address:** You can type the address yourself, or tap the **GPS button** to automatically find and fill in your current location.
5. **Add Visual Evidence:** Tap the camera icon to provide a photo of the incident. You can take a new photo using your camera or choose one from your phone gallery.
6. **Terms and Conditions:** Read the statement at the bottom and click the checkbox to agree to the terms.
7. **Submit:** Click the **Submit Report** button to send the information to the government office.

### Checking Report Status
1. Go to the Check Report Status tab in the bottom navigation menu.
2. Here you will see a list of all reports you have submitted.
3. Each report will have a colored tag:
    * **Pending:** The report is waiting to be seen by an official.
    * **In Progress:** A team is currently working on the issue.
    * **Resolved:** The issue has been fixed.
    * **Rejected:** The report was declined (check the admin remarks for the reason).
4. Tap on any report to see full details and read notes left by the government worker.

## Part 2: Administrator User Manual

This section is for government workers who manage and respond to incoming reports.

### Using the Admin Dashboard
1. Log in with your administrator credentials.
2. The top of your screen shows four cards that count the total work:
    * **Total:** All reports ever submitted.
    * **Resolved:** Reports that are finished.
    * **Pending:** New reports that need your attention.
    * **Rejected:** Reports that were not valid.
3. Below the cards, you will see a list of the most recent reports sent by citizens.

### Managing a Report
1. Click on a report from the list to open the details page.
2. **Review Evidence:** Look at the photo provided by the citizen and check the GPS address.
3. **Update Status:** Use the status menu to change the report's progress. You can move it to "In Progress" when a team is sent, or "Resolved" when the work is done.
4. **Add Remarks:** Type a message in the "Admin Remarks" box. This is where you tell the citizen what is happening (example: "Repair crew has been scheduled for Friday").
5. **Save:** Click the update button to save your changes. The citizen will see the update on their app immediately.

### Searching and Filtering
1. If you need to find an older report, click the "See All" button on the dashboard.
2. You can look through the full history of reports to ensure no neighborhood issue is left ignored.

## Switching to Dark Mode

Both Citizens and Administrators can change the look of the app at any time without leaving their current screen.

1. **Find the Toggle:** Look at the top navigation bar at the very top of your screen. 
2. **Tap to Switch:** You will see a Dark Mode switch that is always accessible across all activities.
3. **Save Your Eyes:** Slide the switch to change the app from a white background to a dark background. This is helpful for using the app at night, reducing eye strain, or saving battery life on your device.
